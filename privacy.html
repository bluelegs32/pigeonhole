Pigeonhole Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Pigeonhole is an iPhone email app. Your email stays between your iPhone and your mail provider, except in the case described under Sharing.

Data Pigeonhole accesses

With permission from the signed-in person, Pigeonhole reads, sends, and organizes that person's email, including messages, senders, recipients, labels, and folders. It connects directly to Google for Gmail and to Apple for iCloud Mail.

Where data is stored

For each email in the inbox and each pinned email, Pigeonhole stores the sender, recipients, subject, preview, date, and status on the iPhone, along with app settings. Full messages and attachments are downloaded when opened and aren't saved by Pigeonhole. Drafts, snoozed emails, follow-up reminders, and addresses the person has sent to (used to suggest recipients) are also stored only on the iPhone. Sign-in credentials are stored in the iPhone's Keychain. Pigeonhole doesn't collect analytics. To show new mail alerts, Pigeonhole checks for mail from the iPhone in the background when iOS allows it, and those alerts are created on the iPhone.

Sharing

Pigeonhole doesn't sell email data or use it for advertising. Email data is shared in one case only: if the person adds their own API key for an AI service, such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini, and taps Summarize with AI or Draft with AI, that conversation is sent to the service they chose to produce the result. Separately, Pigeonhole loads images hosted online when an email is opened, unless this is turned off in Settings, so the servers hosting them can see that the email was opened and from what network address. When the person taps Unsubscribe, Pigeonhole contacts the sender in the way the sender's email asks for: a request to the sender's unsubscribe address, a short email from the person's account, or the sender's own web page.

Widget, badge, and Siri

Pigeonhole's widget and Siri can show the number of new emails and, unless turned off in Settings, the latest senders and subjects. These stay on the iPhone and appear on its Home Screen or Lock Screen. When App Lock is on, only the number is shown.

Tips

Tips and the monthly subscription are handled by Apple through the App Store. Pigeonhole doesn't receive payment details. It only learns whether a purchase went through and whether a subscription is active.

Google user data

Pigeonhole's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Removing an account in Pigeonhole's Settings deletes its sign-in credentials and stored messages from the iPhone. Google access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing Pigeonhole doesn't delete email from the Gmail or iCloud account.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on Pigeonhole's Google sign-in screen.
